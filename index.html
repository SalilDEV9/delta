For Neeharika

In the golden haze of a September morning, I was traveling, wandering through streets I barely knew, when I met her—Neeharika. At first, it was nothing remarkable. Just another online conversation while the world passed by outside my window.

But even in those first two days, I sensed something different about her. She didn’t just speak—she listened. Not the polite nod-and-smile kind of listening, but the kind that notices tremors in voices, the hesitation behind smiles, the silent battles that people carry in their chests.

Her joy is effortless. Not loud or performative, but a quiet, radiant energy that slips past walls. I remember a small moment: a stray paper caught in the wind. “It’s dancing,” she said. “Even chaos can have rhythm.”

Her favorite season is spring, and it suits her perfectly. There’s a rhythm to her presence, a cadence that mirrors renewal itself. Joy, observation, insight—they flow naturally from her.

Because of personal reasons and circumstances, I couldn’t always talk as much as I wanted. But even through all the ups and downs, I feel so comfortable and secure talking to you. That’s rare—and it’s something I treasure deeply.

Happy Birthday, Neeharika. 🌸

May this year be as vibrant, joyful, and full of possibilities as you are. You don't just enter lives—you illuminate them.
